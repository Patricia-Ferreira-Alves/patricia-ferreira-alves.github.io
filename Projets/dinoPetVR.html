DinoPetVR

This prototype of a virtual pet game was made using Unity and the Oculus Integration Pack.

The player has to take care of a dinosaur by targeting its bed, its food bowl or the dinosaur itself in order to fill its hunger, sleep and affection level while they slowly drop with time. The player can pick up some objects and walk around and the dinosaur will follow them.

I also created the assets used in this game using Blender and ZBrush.
