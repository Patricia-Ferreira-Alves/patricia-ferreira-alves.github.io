VR shoot out

This shooting game was made with Unity and the Oculus Integration Pack.

There are four levels in which the player can find a gun and a cube. As the player shoots a cube, it will disappear and another one will appear somewhere randomly around them until the player has shot all the cubes of this level.

Each level works mostly the same but the difference is in the sounds and the haptic signals present in each level. In one level, a cube will make a sound as it appears, therefore giving a clue about its position, and the controller will vibrate if the player is aiming at a cube. One level will have the sounds but no vibrations, one will have the opposite and one will have none.

The goal of this project was to examine the difference in player speed and precision for each situation.
