Dream Traveller

Dream traveller is a VR experience inspired by the topic "Where to dream".

The user is immersed in a maze where each level represents a different dream. They need to find doorways in each level in order to arrive to the final scene.

This VR experience was created for the Oculus Quest 2 using Unity, assets from the Unity store and the Oculus Integration Pack.

The combination of the visuals of the scenes and their sounds are used to create a feeling of curiosity and contemplation while navigating through these places that differ greatly from one another.
