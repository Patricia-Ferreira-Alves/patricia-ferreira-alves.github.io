MVNI

In this game, the player controls a UFO and has to try to reach checkpoints within a time limit.

Obstacles can sometimes appear: trees or other UFOs which have random movements but move slower than the player-controlled one. The UFOs being flying objects, the player can go left, right, up or down. This changes the size of obstacles and of the road in order to give an impression of perspective. It also lets players avoid obstacles but players go faster by staying closer to the road.

The game was programmed in Java and uses the GUI library Swing.
